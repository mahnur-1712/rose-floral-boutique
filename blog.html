How to Keep Your Roses Fresh for Longer

A bouquet of roses is a lovely gift, and nobody wants it drooping after two days. A few small habits canadd days to its life. Here’s what our florists at Rosé Floral Boutique do.

1. Give them a fresh cut

As soon as your bouquet arrives, trim about 2 cm off the bottom of each stem. Cut at an angle, so the stem can’t sit flat against the bottom of the vase and block the water. Use clean, sharp scissors. Blunt ones squash the stem and make it harder to drink.

2. Use a clean vase and clean water

Wash your vase with soap before using it. Old bacteria make the water cloudy and shorten the life of the flowers. Fill it with cool water and add the little flower food packet if your bouquet came with one.

3. Remove the lower leaves

Any leaf that sits under the water will rot and spoil it. Pull off the lower leaves and keep only theones above the waterline.

5. Pick the right spot

Roses like a cool place. Keep them away from:

  • fans and air conditioner vents
  • direct sunlight and hot windows, which matter a lot in our summers
  • a bowl of fruit, because ripening fruit gives off a gas that makes flowers age faster

6. Revive a sleepy rose

If a rose starts to droop, trim the stem and stand it in cool water for an hour or so. Often it perks up again.

A final thought

Flowers are a way of saying “I’m thinking of you”. With a little care, that message lasts longer.
