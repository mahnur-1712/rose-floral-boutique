Returns and Refunds

We want every bouquet to make someone smile. If something goes wrong, we will put it right. Please read this policy before you order.

Our Promise

Every order is made from fresh, carefully chosen flowers. If we made a mistake, or your flowers arrived damaged, we will replace them or refund you. We look into every complaint properly and fairly.

Fresh Flowers

Flowers are perishable, so we cannot accept returns on them. We will replace or refund them only if:

  • we sent the wrong item
  • we made a mistake with your order
  • the flowers arrived badly damaged

How to report a problem

  1. Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Email a clear photo of the whole bouquet and a close-up of the problem to info@rosefloral.co.pk, or call 0513248395.
  3. Give your order number and the delivery address.

Once we have checked your photos, we will arrange a free replacement or a refund as quickly as we can.

Flower Care and Freshness

With good care, most bouquets stay fresh for about 4 to 5 days. This depends on the type of flower, the room temperature, and how often the water is changed and the stems are trimmed. Please take your flowers out of any water pack or wrapping and put them in a vase of clean water as soon as they arrive.

If your flowers wilt earlier than expected, send us a photo and we will look at your case. Any replacement or refund will match the problem that occurred.

Gifts and Other Items

Vases, soft toys and unopened hampers can be returned within 7 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in their original packaging. Opened food items and personalised gifts cannot be returned. Gift cards are not refundable.

To return an item, contact us with your order number. We will arrange collection from the delivery address on a day that suits you, between 9 am and 5 pm.

Delivery Guarantee

If your delivery address is correct and complete, we guarantee your delivery date. If we miss it, you are entitled to a refund.

  • Same-day delivery is available in Wah Cantt for orders placed before 5 PM (PKT).
  • Next-day delivery applies to the rest of Pakistan
  • If nobody is home, our courier will leave a card with a phone number so you can arrange a new delivery. A second attempt may take place the next day, and a Rs. 300 re-delivery fee applies.

Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched, and it costs nothing. Please tell us as early as you can, so we can avoid wasting flowers.

Weddings and Events

Wedding and event orders need careful planning, so they have their own rules:

  • Cancellation 14 days or more before the event: 50% of the deposit is returned.
  • Cancellation 7 to 13 days before the event: 25% of the deposit is returned.
  • Cancellation less than 7 days before the event: the deposit is not returned.

How Refunds Are Paid

We process approved refunds within 5 to 7 working days. The money goes back by the method you paid with (card, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer). For Cash on Delivery orders, we will ask for your wallet or bank details.

We are here from 9 am to 6 pm, 7 days a week.
