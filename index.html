ABOUT Rosé Floral Boutique

Founded in 2012, Rosé Floral Boutique has been a cherished part of the local community for over a decade. What started as a boutique florist in the heart of Islamabad has transformed into a nationwide online retailer, making premium fresh flowers accessible to customers all across Pakistan.

The beautiful bouquets and arrangements you see for sale on our website or in our floral boutiques are the product of both the top-quality flowers we source and the incredible team we have arranging them. Our florists are among the very best in the country, undergoing years of training and practice to create the stunning bouquets we deliver to your doorstep.

Florist Pakistan: Your Destination for Beautiful Bouquets

At Rosé Floral Boutique, we are your trusted luxury florist in Pakistan. Our team of expert florists are masters of their craft, creating stunning floral designs for any occasion across major cities including Islamabad, Lahore, and Karachi. We pride ourselves on crafting every arrangement with passion and precision, ensuring it arrives fresh and looking its absolute best.

Flowers for Every Occasion

Our extensive floral collection is designed to suit any event, so you can always find the perfect flowers to express your feelings:

  • Bouquets: Elegant, hand-tied arrangements crafted with seasonal blooms
  • Roses: Classic premium long-stemmed roses in a variety of vibrant colors.
  • Gift Hampers: Curated luxury hampers featuring flowers, chocolates, and festive treats.
  • Gift Cards: Personalized message cards to add a special touch to your floral gift
  • Wedding Flowers: Bespoke floral arrangements, stage backdrops, and bridal bouquets for your big day.
  • Anniversary Flowers: Mark a special milestone with romantic arrangements tailored to your choice.
  • Sympathy/Funeral Flowers: Thoughtful and respectful tributes to convey your heartfelt condolences.

We also offer flexible options for every budget, providing reliable and affordable fresh flower delivery across Pakistan without compromising on quality.

Q. Do you offer same day delivery?

A. Yes, if you order before 5 PM (PKR time) we will deliver your flowers the same day in Wah cantt and next day for the rest of the Pakistan

Q. What does a florist do?

A. A florist is someone who takes fresh flowers and arranges them into beautiful bouquets. It takes many years of training and practice to become skilled at this job.

Q. Do you offer Sunday flower delivery across Pakistan?

A. Yes. Flower Station operates a full Pakistan delivery service seven days a week, including Sundays and bank holidays, with same-day delivery available for orders placed before 5 PM (PKR time).

Q. Does Flower Station deliver to Pakistan hospitals and corporate offices?

A. Yes. We deliver regularly to corporate headquarters, government offices, private clubs, hotels, and hospitals across Pakistan. Please provide the department name, floor number, or ward details during checkout to ensure smooth delivery.

Q. What happens if the recipient is not at home when the flowers arrive?

A. If the recipient is unavailable, our couriers will leave the flowers with an immediate neighbour or building concierge, or place them in a safe, sheltered location on the property. A calling card and digital notification will be left detailing where the bouquet has been securely placed.
